🌟 Science 6 Review 🌟
Quarter 4 Module 1

🔑 Key Concepts and Main Ideas
  • Earthquakes and volcanic eruptions cause changes on the Earth's surface.
  • Earthquakes result from movements within the Earth's crust.
  • Volcanic eruptions involve the release of magma, gases, ash, and lava.
  • Both natural events can cause destruction to landforms and structures.
📖 Important Definitions
  • Earthquake – A sudden and violent shaking of the ground caused by movements within the Earth's crust.
  • Fault – A break in the Earth's crust where movement occurs.
  • Focus – The origin of an earthquake where it begins.
  • Epicenter – The area directly above the focus.
  • Magma – Molten rocks found beneath the Earth's surface.
  • Lava – Molten rock that flows on the Earth's surface.
  • Volcano – A crack on Earth's surface where lava, steam, and ash escape.
📏 Measurement Scales
  • Richter Scale – Measures the magnitude of an earthquake.
  • Mercalli Scale – Measures the intensity or damage caused by an earthquake.
⚡ Types of Earthquakes
  • Tectonic Earthquake – Caused by sudden movement of tectonic plates.
  • Volcanic Earthquake – Occurs due to volcanic activity.
🌐 Plate Boundaries
  • Convergent – Plates collide.
  • Divergent – Plates move apart.
  • Transform – Plates slide past each other.
🌋 Volcanic Eruption Process
  • Magma forms beneath the Earth's surface.
  • Magma rises and collects in magma chambers.
  • Gas pressure increases.
  • Gases expand as magma rises through the conduit.
  • Magma explodes and exits through the crater.
  • Lava is ejected onto the Earth's surface.
💡 Possible Exam Questions (With Answers)
  • What is the origin of an earthquake? → Focus
  • What scale measures earthquake magnitude? → Richter Scale
  • What molten rock reaches the surface? → Lava
  • Where do tectonic plates collide? → Convergent boundary
  • What happens to gas pressure during eruption? → It increases
